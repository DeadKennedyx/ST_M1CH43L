The cost of noise

17.08.26 / 3 min read

Every signal asks for attention. Few deserve it. The cost of noise is not merely lost time; it is the slow erosion of judgment.

Silence creates enough distance to see what urgency hides. Close the extra window. Let the unanswered notification remain unanswered. Protect the part of the mind that can still recognize what matters.

Attention is not a mood. It is stewardship.
