Discipline over motivation

29.08.26 / 4 min read

Motivation is weather. It arrives with force, changes without permission, and disappears precisely when the work becomes difficult.

Discipline is architecture. It is built once, reinforced daily, and remains standing when the atmosphere turns against you. The smallest honest routine will outlive the grandest intention.

Do not wait to feel ready. Make the next right action so clear and so small that resistance has nowhere to hide. Then return tomorrow.
